# Passages

Two examples passages are included in with the app:
- "The North Wind and the Sun," Aesop, roughly 600 BCE
- "Rain," Robert Louis Stevenson from his 1885 collection *A Child's Garden of Verses*

Other passages are included here, as examples for adding passages to read (Please take care to not infringe on the rights of authors).

- "Gift of the Magi," by O. Henry, 1905
- "Jabberwocky," from <u>Through the Looking Glass</u> by Lewis Carroll, 1871
- "The Happy Prince," "The Selfish Giant," and "The Remarkable Rocket," from <u>The Happy Prince and Other Tales</u> by Oscar Wilde, 1888
- "The Cask of Amontillado," by Edgar Allen Poe, 1846
