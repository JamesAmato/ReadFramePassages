# Passages

Two example passages are included in with the app:

- "The North Wind and the Sun" by Aesop, roughly 600 BCE
- "Rain" by Robert Louis Stevenson from <u>A Child's Garden of Verses</u>, 1885

The public-domain passages below are provided as examples:

- "[Gift of the Magi](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JamesAmato/ReadFramePassages/refs/heads/main/docs/passages/giftofthemagi.txt)" by O. Henry, 1905
- "[Jabberwocky](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JamesAmato/ReadFramePassages/refs/heads/main/docs/passages/jabberwocky.txt)" from <u>Through the Looking Glass</u> by Lewis Carroll, 1871
- "[The Happy Prince](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JamesAmato/ReadFramePassages/refs/heads/main/docs/passages/happy_prince.txt)," "[The Selfish Giant](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JamesAmato/ReadFramePassages/refs/heads/main/docs/passages/selfish_giant.txt)," and "[The Remarkable Rocket](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JamesAmato/ReadFramePassages/refs/heads/main/docs/passages/rocket.txt)" from <u>The Happy Prince and Other Tales</u> by Oscar Wilde, 1888
- "[The Cask of Amontillado](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JamesAmato/ReadFramePassages/refs/heads/main/docs/passages/cask.txt)" by Edgar Allen Poe, 1846
