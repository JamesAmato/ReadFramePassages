# Passages

Two example passages are included in with the app:

- "The North Wind and the Sun," Aesop, roughly 600 BCE
- "Rain," Robert Louis Stevenson from his 1885 collection *A Child's Garden of Verses*

The public-domain passages below are provided as examples:

- "[Gift of the Magi](../passages/giftofthemagi.txt)" by O. Henry, 1905
- "[Jabberwocky](../passages/jabberwocky.txt)" from <u>Through the Looking Glass</u> by Lewis Carroll, 1871
- "[The Happy Prince](../passages/happy_prince.txt)," "[The Selfish Giant](../passages/selfish_giant.txt)," and "[The Remarkable Rocket](../passages/rocket.txt)" from <u>The Happy Prince and Other Tales</u> by Oscar Wilde, 1888
- "[The Cask of Amontillado](../passages/cask.txt)" by Edgar Allen Poe, 1846
