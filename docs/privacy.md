# Read Frame Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

Read Frame does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.

All passages, settings, and other user content are stored locally on the user's device.

Read Frame does not:
- require user accounts
- use analytics services
- display advertising
- track users across apps or websites
- transmit user-created content to external servers

Content imported into Read Frame is processed locally on the device.

If you contact the developer directly for support, any information you voluntarily provide will be used only to respond to your inquiry.
